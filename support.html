Support

Need help with Cubuno? Reach out anytime — we usually reply within 1–2 business days.

How do I tidy photos?

Open Cubuno, pick a time period (or a quick round), then swipe to keep, delete, or favorite. You can switch left-hand mode in Settings.

Does Cubuno upload my photos?

No. Photos stay on your device. We do not upload your photo library to Pandobal servers.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by the store you used. iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Cubuno. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Cubuno. Cancel anytime at least 24 hours before renewal.

I found a bug

Email hello@pandobal.com with your device model, OS version, and a short description. Screenshots help us respond faster.
