How it works

A few swipes. Clearer albums.

Cubuno makes photo tidy feel light — short sessions, playful progress, real control over what stays.

  1. Pick a stretch of time

    Open a month, a week, or a quick random round.

  2. Swipe to decide

    Keep, delete, or favorite — one photo at a time.

  3. Keep the streak

    Earn XP, light your flame, and make tidy a habit.

On your device

Your photos stay yours.

Cubuno organizes the library already on your phone. We don’t run a Cubuno account, and we don’t upload your albums to our servers.
