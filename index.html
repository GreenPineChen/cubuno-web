How to play

Start with Quick Tidy.

Don’t force a massive cleanup. Jump into a short round of about 10 random photos, swipe to keep only what you love, and come back whenever you like.

  1. Quick Tidy first

    The main mode: about 10 photos per round, perfect for spare minutes — jump in anytime.

  2. Swipe to decide

    Keep, delete, or favorite — as effortless as scrolling.

  3. Surprises in Quick Tidy

    Each photo shows how long ago it was taken and where — tidy into moments from different times.

  4. Or tidy by month

    Use month-by-month when you want to work through a specific stretch of memories.

The fun part

More tidy. More XP.

Every decision earns XP (experience points). Light today’s streak flame with one completed round — then keep going whenever you want.
